What if trying to drink less ended with not wanting it at all?

For thirty years I was a dedicated professional drinker. Not a good one, but persistent. Four lost jobs, a first marriage, whole weekends gone. Two ultimatums from my wife, and a Christmas party I don't remember one minute of.

I tried white-knuckling it. Six miserable months, undone by one beach vacation.

All I wanted was control. Two drinks and done. Instead I got not wanting it at all, plus some help from a direction I never saw coming.

Now I still go to the fish fry, the tailgate, and the bar on a Saturday night. Alcohol just doesn't come up anymore. Kind of like bananas. No white-knuckling. No missing it.

This isn't a program. It's my story. Where you land, a Wisconsin few or none at all, is your call.

This book is for you if

  • You want to drink less, or maybe not at all, and you're not sure which yet
  • You've tried gritting your teeth through it and it didn't stick
  • You don't want to give up the fun, the friends, or the Friday fish fry
  • You like your honesty with a side of laughs

About the Author

Brett Allen Bird spent thirty years in a complicated and very public relationship with alcohol. He set out to drink like a normal person and ended up not wanting to drink at all, with some help from a faith he never saw coming. He lives in Wisconsin with his wife, still hits the fish fry and the bar most weekends, and just doesn't drink anymore. He also doesn't eat bananas. That's a different story. Actually, it's the same story. Drink Soberishly is his first book.
